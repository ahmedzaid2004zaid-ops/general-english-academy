Text A

Daniel works in a small office in Chicago. He usually takes the train to work because parking is expensive. Last Monday, the trains were delayed, so he called his manager and explained that he would arrive late. His manager understood and asked him to send an update when he arrived.

Text B

Remote work has changed how many organizations evaluate productivity. While some managers rely on visible activity, such as time spent online, others focus on measurable outcomes. The latter approach can give employees greater autonomy, but it also requires clear objectives, effective communication, and mutual trust. Without these conditions, flexibility may create confusion rather than improve performance.
