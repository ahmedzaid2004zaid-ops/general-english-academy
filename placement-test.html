4. Speaking Interview

Complete this section with your teacher after the written test.

1. Could you introduce yourself and tell me about your daily life?

2. Tell me about a memorable experience you have had.

3. What do you like about the place where you live?

4. Do you prefer studying online or in a classroom? Why?

5. What skill would you like to develop in the next few years, and why?

6. Does technology make life easier or create new problems? Give reasons and examples.
