3. Speaking interview — at the end

Teacher-led interview. Ask the student to answer naturally and give examples. Suggested duration: 8–12 minutes.

Warm-up
1. Could you introduce yourself and tell me a little about your daily life?
Experience and opinions
2. Tell me about a memorable experience you have had.
3. What do you like about the place where you live?
4. Do you prefer studying online or in a classroom? Why?
5. What is one skill you would like to develop in the next few years, and why?
Extended response
6. Some people believe technology makes life easier, while others think it creates new problems. What is your opinion? Give reasons and examples.
